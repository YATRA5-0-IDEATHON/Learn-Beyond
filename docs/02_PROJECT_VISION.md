ac# 02 — Project Vision

## Vision Statement

A Nepal where every student — regardless of geography, family connections, or financial background — can access real professional mentorship, build job-ready skills through real work, and prove their ability with credentials that employers actually trust.

## Mission Statement

MentorDo bridges Nepal's education-employment gap by making the job itself the curriculum: connecting students with verified professionals through real tasks, live mentorship, and a verifiable Skill Passport that replaces the broken degree system with proof of actual ability.

---

## The Change We Want to See

### Before MentorDo
- A student in Dhangadhi studies BBS for 4 years, memorizes textbooks, passes exams, and graduates.
- They apply for 50 jobs. Employers see a degree that proves nothing.
- They get rejected or end up in a job unrelated to their field.
- They leave Nepal for Qatar or Malaysia to find work.

### After MentorDo
- The same student in Dhangadhi opens MentorDo.
- An AI chat asks what they're studying and what they enjoy. It recommends: "Your BBS background + interest in numbers = Junior Accountant path."
- They enroll with a verified CA mentor from Kathmandu.
- Over 3 months, they complete 15 real accounting tasks — ledgers, VAT calculations, financial reports — reviewed by their mentor.
- They pass 3 live certification sessions. Their Skill Passport shows: "Accounting — Expert Certified, verified by Ramesh Sharma, CA, Deloitte Nepal."
- An employer in Kathmandu finds their Skill Passport, verifies the certification in 10 seconds, and offers them a job.
- They didn't need connections. They didn't need to move to Kathmandu to find a mentor. They just needed to do the work.

## Goals

### Short-Term (Prototype / Ideathon)
- Build a working demo of the full student journey: AI onboarding → mentor enrollment → Task Chain → submission → certification → Skill Passport
- Demonstrate the core innovation: real work as curriculum, human verification as quality gate
- Show the business model is viable and scalable

### Medium-Term (6–12 months post-launch)
- Onboard 100 verified mentors across 5 skill categories (Accounting, IT, Business, Design, Healthcare)
- Enroll 1,000 students
- Partner with 10 companies as employer partners
- Pilot with 2 colleges to integrate Task Chains as coursework

### Long-Term (3–5 years)
- Become Nepal's most trusted skill certification platform
- Expand to other South Asian countries with similar education-employment gaps (Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Myanmar)
- Partner with government and international organizations (World Bank, ILO) for rural education programs
- Replace the internship as the standard pathway from education to employment

---

## Design Principles

1. **Real work over theory** — every learning activity is a real professional task, not a lecture or quiz
2. **Human verification over algorithms** — originality and understanding are verified by real professionals in live sessions, not by software that can be gamed
3. **Output credentials over input credentials** — the Skill Passport proves what you can do, not how long you studied
4. **Accessible by design** — works on low bandwidth (Jitsi for video, lightweight web app), affordable session fees (NPR 200–500), available to rural students
5. **Self-sustaining** — the platform earns revenue from session commissions and employer partnerships, not grants or charity, so it can keep running and improving
